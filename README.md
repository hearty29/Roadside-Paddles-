Roadside Paddles - Food Delivery Apps 

All-in-One Food Delivery, Courier & Business Management Platform

Roadside Paddles is a modern, full-featured food delivery and business management platform designed to give businesses their own dedicated ordering and delivery system.

Instead of depending entirely on third-party delivery platforms, FlavorFleet allows businesses to manage their customers, menu, orders, delivery riders, payments, and reservations from one centralized platform.

The system is designed to be flexible and customizable according to the needs of each business.

🛵 Courier / Rider System

FlavorFleet includes a dedicated delivery rider system.

Rider Application

Potential riders can submit an application containing:

- Personal information
- Contact details
- Vehicle type
- Plate number
- Identification information

Administrators can review applications and approve or reject riders.

Rider Dashboard

Approved riders can:

- View available delivery orders
- Accept delivery requests
- View customer information
- Contact customers directly
- Call customers with one tap
- Send pre-filled SMS messages
- Mark orders as Picked Up
- Mark orders as Delivered
- Receive sound notifications for new deliveries

---

👨‍💼 Admin Management Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard provides centralized control over the entire platform.

Order Management

Administrators can:

- View incoming orders in real time
- Receive new-order sound notifications
- Manage order statuses
- Monitor active deliveries
- Manage cancelled orders
- Monitor completed orders

Menu Management

Administrators can:

- Add menu items
- Edit menu items
- Remove products
- Change prices
- Update descriptions and photos
- Mark products as Available
- Mark products as Sold Out

Rider Management

Administrators can:

- Review rider applications
- Approve riders
- Reject applications
- Manage rider permissions
- Manage administrator and rider roles

🏓 Pickleball Hub

FlavorFleet can also support businesses that offer pickleball courts and reservations.

Court Management

- Multiple court support
- Live court status

- Surface type
- Lighting information
- Day and night hourly rates

- Countdown timer for active games
