---
🍽️ Food ordering
- Browse food by category and manage a shopping cart.
 - Choose Delivery or Counter / Walk-in.
- Enter contact details, delivery address, and order notes.
- Track preparation, pickup, delivery, or counter collection.
- View order history and contact the assigned rider.

🛵 Rider management
Submit rider applications with vehicle and license information.
Upload profile photos and private identification documents.
Accept available delivery orders and update delivery status.
Upload required proof-of-delivery photos.
Contact customers through calls, SMS, or chat.
View completed deliveries and receive pickup alerts.

🛡️ Admin dashboard
View daily order counts, active orders, and completed-order revenue.
Manage delivery and counter orders.
Update preparation statuses and mark counter orders as claimed.
Add, edit, delete, and control menu availability.
Approve or reject rider applications.
Manage support conversations and tournaments.

🏓 Pickleball court booking
View court availability and day/night rates.
Select courts, dates, and hourly slots.
Add equipment rentals and contact details.
Choose counter payment or submit GCash details and receipts.
Admins can review bookings, block slots, and free up slots.

🏆 Tournament system
Create doubles tournaments for 2–32 teams.
Choose single or double elimination.
Register teams with team names and player information.
Approve, reject, edit, or withdraw registrations.
Generate brackets with automatic byes and advancement.
Display green Winner, red Loser, and gold Championship brackets.
Record scores and assign match courts and schedules.
Correct results or move teams with confirmation and recorded history.
Display final placements and winning teams.
View a dark live bracket with glowing connections, scorecards, a champion panel, and expanded viewing.

🎁 Custom tournament prizes
Create any number of custom awards.
Add cash amounts, descriptions, and non-cash rewards.
Support Philippine Peso and other configured currencies.
Assign placement prizes automatically.
Assign special awards to teams or individual players.
Display prizes before registration and alongside final results.
Edit prizes before, during, or after the tournament.

📺 Tournament livestreams — new
Configure Facebook or YouTube broadcasts during tournament creation or editing.
Set Scheduled, Live, or Ended status manually.
Display an embedded video player and direct Watch Live link.
Add an optional replay link.
Update or remove broadcast settings without recreating the tournament.
The admin broadcasts through the smartphone’s Facebook or YouTube app, then adds the video link to the tournament.

💬 Accounts and messaging
Login and registration with customer, rider, and admin roles.
Customer–admin, rider–admin, and customer–rider chats.
Conversation search, unread indicators, and open/resolved filters.
Resolve or reopen conversations.
Delete for me hides a conversation from that account; new messages restore it.

🎨 Navigation and layout
Visible text labels for every header navigation icon.
Centered Roadside Paddles branding.
Responsive layouts for phones and tablets.
